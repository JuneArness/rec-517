About REC 517

Positive energy, built for Jackson.

REC 517 LLC was founded in 2026 to give the Jackson region a premier, accessible home for indoor sports, training and community activity.

517

Mission and vision

“

Our mission

To build positive energy in the community by providing accessible sports, training, and recreational opportunities.
“

Our vision

To become Jackson’s premier indoor sports and community activity center.

Why REC 517

Six reasons, one roof.

REC 517 combines athletic development, recreation, youth development, education, community and economic opportunity in a single facility.

Athletic development

Skill development, team training and competition across basketball, volleyball, futsal, pickleball and golf.

Recreation

Open gym, community leagues and weekend family recreation, indoors and year-round.

Youth development

Programs for ages 4–18 built around teamwork, leadership and positive values.

Education

Programming that supports academic performance, plus health and wellness workshops.

Community

Space and programs for families, schools, churches and local organizations.

Economic opportunity

Projected construction and permanent jobs, local partnerships and visitors for regional events.

The need

Jackson needs somewhere to play when the weather won’t cooperate.

The REC 517 business plan finds strong demand for structured recreation in the Jackson region, with limited year-round, weather-independent options and no comparable multi-sport facility within a 30-mile radius.

Market findings are from the REC 517 business plan.

Our goals

  • Establish REC 517 as the leading indoor sports complex in the region within three years.
  • Engage at least 3,000 community members every year through diverse programs and events.
  • Form strategic partnerships with local schools, organizations and sponsors to broaden our reach and impact.

Our partner

Partnered with the Hollywood Golf Institute.

REC 517 LLC is a partner of the Hollywood Golf Institute, founded by Selina “Hollywood” Johnson in 1980 and incorporated in Detroit in 1984. Known as “The First Lady of Golf,” Johnson has introduced more than 4,000 young people to the game, and more than 150 of her students have earned collegiate scholarships.

REC 517’s plan includes a dedicated golf training area with youth instruction.
