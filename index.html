Who we are

A home for sports, training and community in Jackson.

REC 517 LLC is planning a multi-sport indoor recreational and training facility dedicated to serving Jackson, Michigan and the surrounding region.

The plan brings athletic programming, youth development and community engagement together under one roof, designed to foster positive energy, accessibility and growth. The goal: become Jackson’s premier destination for sports and community activities, driven by a commitment to excellence and inclusivity.

Our approach

Built for more than competition.

REC 517’s programs are designed to boost academic performance, teamwork and leadership, so young people leave with more than a better game.

Youth development

Structured programs for ages 4–18, from first skills to competitive play.

Teamwork

Leagues, team training and group programs where kids learn to win together.

Leadership

Coaching and mentorship that builds leaders on and off the court.

Character

Focus, motivation and discipline, grounded in positive values.

Education

Programming designed to support academic performance, not compete with it.

Community engagement

Open houses, youth clinics, school visits and annual community events.

Accessibility

Accessible, inclusive, year-round recreation, whatever the weather.

Opportunity

New jobs, new partnerships and new pathways for Jackson’s young people.

The facility

About 70,500 square feet, planned for Jackson.

A year-round, weather-independent home for sport in a region the business plan identifies as lacking large-scale indoor space for tournaments, training and community events.

  • About 50,500 sq. ft. for basketball, volleyball and dance competition courts
  • About 20,000 sq. ft. for golf training and equipment
  • Training rooms, common areas, meeting space and offices
