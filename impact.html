Community impact

More than a sports facility.

REC 517 is planned to open doors for young people, create jobs and bring new energy to Jackson, Michigan.

Youth development

Better athletes. Stronger young people.

Youth ages 4–18 are at the heart of the plan, with programs designed to boost academic performance, teamwork and leadership.

The goal, in the plan’s own words, is to strengthen young people’s “character, focus, and motivation.”

Workforce

Jobs from groundbreaking to game day.

The business plan projects jobs in two phases: building the facility, then running it.

Construction phase · projected

100+ jobs

The plan prioritizes hiring local contractors and tradespeople, including electricians, plumbers, HVAC technicians, masons and general laborers, through partnerships with area unions and workforce development programs.

Operating phase · projected

20+ permanent

Plus 20–30 part-time and seasonal positions, including:

  • Coaches, trainers and youth program staff
  • Teachers and vocational trainers
  • Fitness instructors and nutrition educators
  • Facility management, maintenance and customer service
  • Event and outreach coordinators, concessions staff

All job figures are projections from the REC 517 business plan.

REC 517 envisions a $5,000,000 investment to build a facility that strengthens young people, families, schools and the local economy.

What’s next

The growth plan.

Once the facility is established, the plan calls for growing programs and reach, supported by grant funding and corporate partnerships.

  1. Golf simulators

    Acquire golf simulators to complement golf training.

  2. Travel teams

    Launch travel teams for competitive youth athletes.

  3. After-school programs

    Add after-school programs and special events.

  4. More training space

    Expand training through community partners, churches and neighboring school facilities.

  5. Concessions & retail

    Add concessions and retail in nearby vacant space.

  6. Satellite programs

    Develop programs in neighboring communities.
