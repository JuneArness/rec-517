Planned facility

Coming to Jackson: 70,500 sq. ft. of play.

A planned multi-sport indoor facility with courts for basketball, volleyball and dance competition, plus a dedicated golf training area, open year-round whatever the Michigan weather.

Site location and opening date to be announced.

The layout

Two zones, one destination.

Most of the building is planned for courts and competition, with a dedicated golf training area alongside.

The court zone is planned for basketball, volleyball and dance competition. The golf zone covers training and equipment, and the growth plan adds golf simulators to complement it.

Facility design schematics will be shared when available.

Inside REC 517

Planned spaces.

Every space below comes from the REC 517 business and operations plans.

Multi-sport courts

Courts for basketball, volleyball, futsal and pickleball.

Dance & competition space

Part of the planned 50,500 sq. ft. court and competition area.

Golf training area

About 20,000 sq. ft. for golf training and equipment.

Turf space

Turf for indoor soccer and futsal, available for team rentals.

Training rooms

Space for personal and team training programs.

Seating & locker rooms

Spectator seating and locker rooms for teams and events.

Meeting rooms

Rentable rooms for teams, schools, organizations and businesses.

Common areas & concessions

Gathering space for families, spectators and visitors.

Offices

Administration and program offices on site.

Rentals & events

Host your team, school or event here.

Court, turf and meeting-room rentals are planned for schools, churches, organizations, businesses and community groups.

  • Team practices and league play
  • Tournaments and weekend events
  • Team building, charity events, business expos and community festivals
